Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026
⚠ Template — review with a New Zealand lawyer before going live. Drafted to reflect the Privacy Act 2020 but not legal advice.

Offsider is operated by Emporom Media (part of the Empora Group), Wellington, New Zealand. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the Privacy Act 2020.

1. Who we are

Offsider ("we", "us") provides automated back-office software for trades businesses — missed-call response, lead follow-up, invoicing and accounting sync. Contact: hello@offsider.co.nz.

2. Information we collect

  • Account & business details — your name, business name, email, phone, and trade.
  • Customer interaction data — when you connect your business number, we process inbound calls and SMS so we can reply on your behalf, plus the contact details and messages of people who contact you.
  • Accounting data — where you connect Xero or MYOB, we access invoice and payment data needed to draft and reconcile invoices.
  • Usage data — basic, privacy-respecting analytics on how the product is used.

3. How we use it

To operate the service: respond to missed calls, send follow-ups, draft and chase invoices, sync with your accounting software, and support you. We do not sell your data or your customers' data.

4. Sharing

We share data only with the providers needed to run the service (e.g. SMS/telephony, accounting APIs, hosting), under agreements that require them to protect it. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Storage & security

Data is stored securely and access is limited to what's needed to run Offsider. We retain information only as long as needed to provide the service or meet legal obligations.

6. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to access or correct your personal information, and ask us to delete your account data. Email hello@offsider.co.nz.

7. Your customers

As an Offsider user you're responsible for having a lawful basis to contact your customers. Offsider helps you respond to people who contact you first, and includes opt-out handling consistent with the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007.

8. Changes

We'll update this policy as the product evolves and note the date above.
