Usage
Do
✓Keep clear space around the mark (≥ half its height).
✓Wordmark: "Off" in ink/white, "sider" in accent blue.
✓Use the mark on graphite or white only.
✓Numbers & labels in Space Mono.
Don't
✕Recolour the gradient or stretch the mark.
✕Put the mark on busy photos or low-contrast colours.
✕Use SaaS-slop words ("seamless", "revolutionise").
✕Mix in other fonts.
