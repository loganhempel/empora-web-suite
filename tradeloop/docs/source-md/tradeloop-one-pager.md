# TradeLoop

**The back office that runs itself — for trades.**

You're on the tools all day. TradeLoop handles the office while you're there: it answers the calls you miss, follows up the quotes that go quiet, sends the invoices the same day, and keeps your books straight. You just check the two-minute morning rundown.

---

## What it does

- **Answers every missed call in 47 seconds.** A text goes back in your voice and books the job — before they ring the next tradie. 78% of people book whoever replies first.
- **Chases your quotes and old customers.** Automatic 7-day follow-ups bring back work that would've gone cold.
- **Invoices the same day.** Job done → invoice drafted with parts and labour → you tap approve → it's sent and synced to Xero. No more Sunday-night admin.
- **Guards your margins.** Every quote is checked against real cost. Anything thin gets flagged before it goes out — not after the parts bill lands.

Tradies miss roughly **a third of their calls**, and most of those callers just ring someone else. Late invoices cost Kiwi small businesses **$827m a year**. One recovered job a month covers TradeLoop several times over.
