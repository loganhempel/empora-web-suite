Scripts for landing the first 10 clients. Built around the research: lead with the missed-call audit, talk in jobs and dollars, NZ vernacular, one ask. Start with your warm list — Fantail HVAC, Ultra Fast Pest, Singh, 2in1/Welly Lawns — then work the local trade circle.

## 1. The missed-call audit (the opener that does the selling)

Ring the tradie's business number twice during work hours. If it rings out to voicemail, that's your whole pitch.

**If it goes to voicemail — leave this, or text it:**

> Hey [name], it's Logan from Offsider. Just tried your number twice and got voicemail — no worries, you're flat out. That's exactly the problem we fix: when you can't pick up, we text the customer back in under a minute and book the job before they ring the next bloke. Worth a 10-min look? I'll show you on your own numbers.

**If they pick up:**

> "Quick one — when you're up a ladder or under a house and the phone goes, what happens to that call?" *(let them answer — it's usually voicemail)* "Right, so how many of those do you reckon you lose a week?" *(they'll guess low)* "We text them back in 47 seconds in your voice and book the job. Want me to show you?"

## 2. Cold email sequence (3 touches)

Keep each under 120 words, one ask, real personalisation, NZ opt-out. Send from a real person, plain text.

### Email 1 — the miss

**Subject:** tried your number twice, [first name]

> Hey [first name],
>
> Rang [business] twice this morning about [specific: a heat pump / your drainage work] and got voicemail both times. Not a dig — you're on the tools, that's the job.
>
> But that's a customer who's now ringing the next name on Google. Offsider fixes exactly that: miss a call, we text them back in under a minute in your voice and book the job. Same system sends your invoices same-day and chases the late ones.
>
> Worth a 10-minute look on your own numbers?
>
> Logan

>
> Logan · *STOP to opt out.*
