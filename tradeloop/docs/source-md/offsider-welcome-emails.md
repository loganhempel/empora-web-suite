# Offsider — Welcome & Onboarding Email Sequence

The lifecycle emails for a new Offsider client — from signup to live to advocate. Human tone, NZ, one clear next step each. Send from a real person (Logan), plain text.

---

## Email 1 — Welcome (immediately after signup / deposit)

**Subject:** You're in, [first name] — here's what happens next

> Welcome aboard, [first name]. Offsider's going to start catching the calls, quotes and invoices you've been losing — but first, a quick setup.
>
> **Three things I'll need on our call (15 min):**
> 1. Access to forward your business number
> 2. Your Xero (or MYOB) login
> 3. How you talk + your rough pricing
>
> Grab a time here: [booking link]. We'll have you live within the week.
>
> — Logan

## Email 2 — Setup booked / pre-call (after they book)

**Subject:** Quick prep for Thursday

> Cheers [first name] — locked in for [day/time]. Nothing to prepare, but if you can have your Xero login handy and a rough idea of your standard job prices, we'll fly through it.
>
> On the call I'll show you Offsider answering a test call live, so you'll see exactly how it sounds before it talks to a single customer.
>
> — Logan

## Email 3 — You're live (day of go-live)

**Subject:** Offsider's on. 🎯

> [first name] — you're live. From now:
> - Miss a call → Offsider texts them back in under a minute, in your voice
> - Job done → invoice drafted + synced to Xero
> - Each morning → a two-minute digest of everything it did
>
> That digest is the only thing you need to check. Read every thread anytime, and jump in whenever you want.
>
> I'll check in at the end of week one. Go get on the tools.
>
> — Logan

## Email 4 — First-week check-in (day 7)

**Subject:** First week — here's what Offsider did

> [first name], one week in. So far Offsider has:
> - Caught **[X] missed calls** → **[Y] booked**
> - Sent **[Z] invoices** (avg [N]h after the job)
> - Recovered roughly **$[amount]**
>
> Anything sound off in the replies? Tell me and I'll tune the tone. Otherwise — it's just going to keep doing this.
>
> — Logan

## Email 5 — Monthly value report (ongoing, day 30+)

**Subject:** Your month with Offsider — $[amount] recovered

> [first name], your month in numbers:
> - **$[amount]** recovered (jobs caught + faster invoicing)
> - **[X]** jobs booked from missed calls
> - **[Y]** dead quotes revived
> - Avg rating now **[4.9★]** from auto review requests
>
> That's [N]× what Offsider costs you. Keep it cooking.
>
> P.S. Know another tradie drowning in calls? Send them my way — I'll sort them out and there's a month on us for you both.
>
> — Logan

---

## Notes
- Personalise the numbers from the dashboard — real figures are the whole point.
- Keep it plain-text and short; tradies read on their phone between jobs.
- Email 5 doubles as your referral engine — the value report is the natural moment to ask.
- Comply with UEMA: clear sender, easy unsubscribe on any marketing sends.
